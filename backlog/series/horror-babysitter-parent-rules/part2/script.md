# The Dad Was Counting Outside the Door (Pt2)

**Premise (this part).** Seconds after Part 1: something with Mr. Doyle's voice is counting to five outside the door Ivy just refused to open. It knocked, which rule three says the Doyles never do, but it knew the password. The peephole shows an empty hall; the voice comes from below the lens. Ivy sends Mrs. Doyle a photo of the fridge note and finds the 9:40 text she missed: both parents are still at dinner across town. At five the door goes quiet, and the baby monitor crackles: asleep, facing the wall, Theo says where Ivy is standing. Rule four says he isn't talking to her. On the monitor, Theo is holding the Extension 2 receiver, and a long grey hand from under the bed is holding it with him. The talk light comes on, "Ivy? I'm scared. Come get me," and Ivy obeys rule one and stays put. Then Mrs. Doyle answers: her husband is right there with her, and she only wrote three rules. Rules one and four, the two in pencil, were the ones keeping Ivy out of Theo's room. "Get Theo. Get out." Down the hall, Theo's door clicks shut.

**Mechanic (one thing the viewer can track).** The count, one to five, is the clock, and each number lands on a new fact: 1 the knock tell, 2 the empty peephole, 3 the unread text, 4 the missed 9:40 text, 5 the silence. After five the threat moves from outside the door to inside Theo's room, and the note's own rules, which kept Ivy safe from the door, now keep her away from the child.

**Plants and payoffs.**

- **Paid from Part 1:** the frame-0 lock-screen preview "Mrs. Doyle · 9:40 PM: Still at dinner! Back by 11:30 x" (paid 0:25.1, 32%); the grey pencil rules 1 and 4 (paid 1:10.1, 89%); "EXT 2 · THEO'S ROOM" on the handset (paid 0:49.8, 63%).
- **Planted here for Part 3 (unpaid):** the chewed blue pencil stub clipped to the note's magnet (1:10.1); the receiver's cord running under the bed (0:52.8); "He's right here with me" (two Doyles accounted for; pays in Part 5); Theo's door shut from inside (1:15.6). Fair play (BIBLE §4): the thing can shut any door but can never open one, so the shut door is its move and Ivy will have to open it herself in Part 3.
- **Rule three** is the hook's contradiction (0:04.9) and the series' last proof (Part 5); here it only tells Ivy the door voice isn't family.
- **The count** gives the viewer a progress bar: they know the part ends around "five", and it doesn't (the turn comes after it).

**Loops.** Closed in this part: who is at the door (not Mr. Doyle: he's at dinner, 0:27-0:30, then confirmed 1:06); where the parents are (dinner, 0:25); can the note be trusted (no, rules 1 and 4 are forged, 1:10). Open at the cut (4): what is under the bed; who wrote the pencil rules; why Theo's door shut; can Ivy get him out.
