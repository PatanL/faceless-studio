# It Used My Mother's Voice (Part 2/5)

**Premise of this part.** The third knock lands and the voice at June's door is Odette's, asking for her unit. Rule four says that means it isn't Odette. The real Odette texts from under her bed on floor three: it's at her door too, using June's voice. The voice changes to Dale (whose stair photos all say floor six), to Walt the super, and to June's mother, using the pet name only her mother uses. June calls her real mother, and the door repeats her mother's words half a second later; the call drops. In the peephole stands June herself. It leans in and, in her voice, starts "Hi, this is June, from Seven" and stops, waiting for her to finish.

**Series line restated for cold viewers by 17 s:** "Typing it marks you. Saying it opens." (Odette, P2 0:14.2). Hidden law 3 (BIBLE §5A) is what the cliff dramatises: the Office can start the sentence in any voice but only the resident can finish it.
