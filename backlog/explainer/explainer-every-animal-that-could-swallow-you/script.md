# Every Ocean Giant Ranked By Whether It Could Swallow You

**Premise (one line):** Every big-mouthed ocean giant gets one test: a red ring the width of your shoulders (about 40 cm, one large pizza box). We rank them on a board from "not even close" to "you fit", and the answer at the top is a twist: the only throat that might fit you has never been caught with a person in it, but keeps being found full of our rubbish.

**Engine:** PLAYBOOK §1a ladder/countdown variant. Hook H2 (title as an action on you), with an ERAS-style teaser contract and board reveal. Two-axis ladder (§4): **throat width vs the gauge** (mug, about 10 cm → grapefruit/plate → about 38 cm → stops at the shoulders → on paper, maybe) × **hypothetical to real** (imagined gulp → documented engulfings → filmed shark-swallowing → the giant that could, and what it has really swallowed). The verdict curve is a sawtooth: the biggest animal ever opens at the bottom, the basking shark drops back to the bottom row at 34%, then the climb resumes. Twist (§5): villain swap plus the opening rule turned around ("no gear" ↔ stomachs full of nets and rope).

**What is ours, not borrowed:** the shoulder-gauge ring and pizza-box unit; the ocean-only lineup (blue whale, whale shark, basking shark, megamouth, humpback, goliath grouper, sperm whale); the bomb-carbon whale shark, the Zuiyo-maru "sea monster", the megamouth sea anchor, humpbacks breaking up orca hunts, the grouper's shark gulp and harvest ban; the plastic-stomach turn and "no gear" payoff; every line of wording.

**Board (tier rows, bottom to top, red to green):** 1 NOT EVEN CLOSE · 2 MOUTH YES, THROAT NO · 3 RETURNED TO SENDER · 4 STUCK AT THE SHOULDERS · 5 YOU FIT (the sperm whale card sits here with a "?" badge and the stamp ON PAPER, MAYBE).
