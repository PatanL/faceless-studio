# Title options: explainer-never-meet-orca

Ranked best first. Patterns are taken from the explainer corpus (`research/corpus/explainer/INDEX.json`, PLAYBOOK §7) and the library title analysis (`research/library/index_notes/explainer.md`, "Titles" row): the outliers use second person in 50% of titles, frame personal danger or a negation, and 64% are questions or curiosity frames, with no caps words. Search demand for the subject is in `backlog/explainer/TOPICS.md` row 1 (WILD VERSUS 56M at 83x, OctoLab 8.3M at 319x).

Two constraints shaped the ranking:
- **No duplicate of the pilot.** The 9:16 pilot `episodes/explainer-orca` already uses "Why You'd Never Want To Meet An Orca", and its alt title is "Orcas Could Hunt You. They Choose Not To." The long-form needs its own title (originality review). Option 1 is close to the pilot's *alt* title, which never shipped, so the pilot keeps its main title and is described as a cut-down of this video.
- **The title must agree with the ending.** The video concludes that you are safe because orcas decided you are not food, and ends on the sting that this rests on a habit. A pure-danger title ("never want to meet") fights that conclusion. A paradox title sets it up.
