# Title options: whatif-breathe-underwater

Ranked best first. Patterns are the hook/title formulas in `research/corpus/whatif/PLAYBOOK.md` §2 and the title analysis in `research/library/index_notes/whatif.md` (every outlier title is a question; half contain a number). Guardrails for every option:

- Don't upload under the bare same-premise title "What If You Could Breathe Underwater?". Peekaboo (2.35M) and What If / Underknown (1.59M) already own it, and Ifsphere has 250K on it (TOPICS.md row 12). Using it invites "copy of X" comments and gives up search differentiation (originality critique, PLAYBOOK §10C fatigue).
- Keep the search phrase "breathe underwater" where we can, and lead with our own angle: the one-way trip, the depth, or the twist that your own body is the problem.
- The title card in the video (WHAT IF YOU COULD BREATHE UNDERWATER?, spoken at 0:32) is the first clause of option 1, so the title and the video agree word for word.
