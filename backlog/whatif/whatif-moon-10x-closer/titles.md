Patterns are the hook formulas in `research/corpus/whatif/PLAYBOOK.md` §2 and the title analysis in `research/library/index_notes/whatif.md`: every outlier title is a question, and 50% carry a number, against 14% of controls. Guardrails for every option: keep "Moon" and a number in the first five words, use no "disappeared" (the @man_ia_c signature verb, and the wrong verb here), and make no claim the video doesn't pay off. Option 1 is used exactly on the title card and on the end-card repeat.

Premise risk (PLAYBOOK §10C): "Moon closer" is a familiar what-if, and familiar premises underperform in this template (Earth stops spinning 0.46×, gravity off 0.86×). The title can't fix that on its own. What does is the frame-0 cover (a giant Moon over the White Cliffs with a live counter) and the exact, surprising numbers inside (walk to France, satellites flung, the Moon falling). So the best titles keep the keyword short and let the cover carry the specifics.
