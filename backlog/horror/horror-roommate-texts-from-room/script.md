# My Roommate Texted Me: Don't Come Out

**Premise.** At 11:40 my roommate Dani texts me from her bedroom: don't come out, the girl in the kitchen
has her face. The kitchen girl, in Dani's exact voice, calls me to come eat. The texting Dani gives two rules;
the voice comes to my door and says the texter is the copy, using Dani's phone. I break rule two to text our
landlord, who says Dani moved out yesterday and her room is empty. A photo arrives from that empty room.
Her door clicks twice, my handle turns, and the thread calmly gives rule three: dinner is for three.

**Ownable signature:** Dani's bedroom door clicks twice when it opens (planted 0:15, silent all night, pays off 1:23.8).
**Countable plant:** "enough for all of us" (0:18.8) in a two-person flat, paid off as "dinner is for three" (1:29.0).
**Rules:** rule one, don't answer her (kept); rule two, hold your handle and she can't open it (broken 1:04.8, punished 1:26.0); rule three arrives from the wrong mouth in the last line.

Master loop: which Dani is real? Opens 0:04.3, restated 0:59.5, answered "neither" at 1:11.8.
Micro loops: why no click (0:17.4 -> 1:23.8), "all of us" (0:18.8 -> 1:29.0), does rule two hold (0:26.3 -> 1:26.0), can I get out the window (0:35.7 -> 0:38.0), who has Dani's phone (0:53.5 -> 1:20.0, deepened, left open).
Open at the cut (3): who sent the photo from an empty room, where the real Dani went, what happens when I come out. That is one above the standalone band (0-2), on purpose: it feeds comments and leaves a Part 2 door.
