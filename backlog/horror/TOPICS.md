# Horror backlog — topic selection

Niche: phone/group-chat apartment horror in 2D cartoon (mraevq / udhwkz format). Each episode has a chat-screen hook, a set of rules or warnings, a fake-authority twist and a cliffhanger. Length 60–180 s, 9:16. Four of the picks are 2-part series.
Selected 2026-11-01 ~09:05 UTC. View counts come from yt-dlp metadata read today.

## Evidence used

- `research/library/candidates-horror.json`: 21 outlier and control videos with channel medians (mraevq, udhwkz, lehwh7, twezed, Wansee, WrONG Rules, Texty Stories, Horror Shorts Party).
- `research/R2/transcripts/cap_*.txt`, `panel_*.txt`, plus the R2 hook OCR on the Spark (`~/studio/refs/R2/outliers/hook_*.json`). These show what the top outliers are about. **udhwkz 2.2M (63x)** and **695K (20x)** both open on *"unspoken rules for night-shift truckers… tonight I broke them."* **mraevq 428K (61x)** opens on *"Dad dragged me outside… everyone in our complex died in their sleep."* **mraevq 194K (27.6x)** is a dorm supervisor's warning, "your roommate isn't human", followed by a rule.
- R2 channel listings on the Spark (`~/studio/refs/R2/meta/ytc_*.jsonl`, `user_*.jsonl`): ChenJie Horror, lehwh7, twezed, The Story Vault, It's Chinese Slop, Resident Guidance, Textify, Horror Shorts Party, topscaryclips, melovebz.
- New checks for this file, run on the Spark with `~/.local/bin/yt-dlp --flat-playlist`: 32 `ytsearch20/25:` topic queries, plus channel tabs for WrONG Rules (shorts and videos), The Rule Files, Unfiled, Wansee shorts and Texty Stories shorts. The raw JSONL is copied to `backlog/horror/evidence/` (1.8 MB) and is also in `dgx-spark:~/studio/refs/topics_horror/`.
- No R2 `report.md` and no `research/corpus/horror/` existed when this file was written. The S1 or backlog writers should add corpus references when those land.
- `episodes/` does not exist yet, so none of these duplicates an existing episode.
- **Excluded on purpose:** the seed story behind both reference TikToks (a killer in the complex, a fake cop at the door, a fake building manager who mutes the chat, a burner phone). Our picks can use the general "authority account in the chat" device, but must not reuse that plot or those beats.

**What the checks say:**
1. **Rules and taboo hooks win the biggest multiples in this exact format.** udhwkz's two trucker-rules videos are its #1 and #2 (63x and 20x). WrONG Rules' "New Household Rules (Pt3?)" did 103K, 11x its Shorts median. Its "Mom's Note Day 1/2/???" series did 290K, 202K and 187K, every part above the 65K median. Resident Guidance's "If… Don't…" titles hit 5–17x on a small channel.
2. **"Don't trust your family's voice" is the biggest text-horror theme.** Textify: "Don't Answer Your Mom's Voice" 2.74M, "Do Not Answer Your Family's Voice" 2.46M, "Your Family Isn't Real" 764K, against a 17.5K median. The Rule Files: "If Your Dog Barks At Your Mom On FaceTime… HANG UP" 66K (5.7x).
3. **Text-chat horror is a proven big format by itself.** Luke Davidson's "THE SCARIEST TEXT CHAT EVER!!!" got 16.2M and 9.0M. SSSniperWolf's "MONICA" creepy texts got 18.0M. Mr. Nightmare's "Text Conversations with Disturbing Backstories" got 4.2M.
4. **Everyday-service settings carry a topic premium:** food delivery (Mr. Nightmare 5.45M and 4.31M, Martin Animations 2.22M), emergency alerts (2.48M; Mr. Creeps 492K and 310K), elevators ("The Elevator Game" 7.4M; Unfiled "Floor 13" 95K, 4.6x).
5. **Series work, and the payoff part often wins.** WrONG Rules Pt3 got 11x while Pt2 got 1.0x. topscaryclips' water park story part 2/3 got 498K (121x), while parts 1 and 3 got 10–14x. So we end Part 1 on an unanswered question and title Part 2 so it can be found on its own.
6. Sub-genres with weak numbers: smart-home horror (every search result under 4K views) and "neighbors disappearing from the group chat" as a title (QUIET ALARM, 129 views). This is why candidate 14 was dropped and candidate 8 is built on a different hook.

## The 10 picks (in production order)

Each one-line note gives comparable videos with their views.

1. **horror-night-doorman-rules** (2-part): the highest outlier multiples in our exact format are "rules I was taught, tonight I broke them" stories: udhwkz trucker rules 2.2M (63x) and 695K (20x). Mr. Nightmare night shift 1.76M.
2. **horror-mom-two-chats** (2-part): "don't trust your family's voice" is the largest text-horror theme. Textify "Don't Answer Your Mom's Voice" 2.74M and "Do Not Answer Your Family's Voice" 2.46M (~140x a 17.5K median).
3. **horror-new-building-rules** (2-part series): rules notices in rules-horror Shorts. WrONG Rules "New Household Rules (Pt3?)" 103K (11x), Mr. Creeps apartment-rules 873K, lehwh7 "Strange rules appeared in our apartment complex" 26K.
4. **horror-two-emergency-alerts**: two conflicting alerts are a fake-authority twist that is pure UI. EAS horror 2.48M (GregBroDudeMan); Mr. Creeps curfew alert 492K and "every screen: HIDE" 310K.
5. **horror-roommate-texts-from-room**: "my roommate isn't human" is a top outlier on two of the format's accounts. mraevq 194K (27.6x), melovebz 134K (23.8x).
6. **horror-delivery-photo-inside**: food-delivery horror is one of the biggest everyday-setting topics. Mr. Nightmare 5.45M and 4.31M, Martin Animations 2.22M, Horror Shorts Party Amazon delivery 728K (2.9x).
7. **horror-text-from-tomorrow**: a warning about something that hasn't happened yet drives the biggest mraevq story (Dad's warning, 428K, 61x). lehwh7 "note says the train crashes in 2 minutes" 508K.

## Notes for script writers

- **Hook (first 3 s):** open on the chat screen with the rule or warning as the first line, like udhwkz's "Every trucker knows you never…". Within 5–8 s, cut to the narrator character and the line "but tonight I…".
- **Fake authority:** every pick has an official-looking account (owner, Management, alert system, app support, maintenance, "night security", the landlord). The reveal must come from a second, more trustworthy channel. Don't reuse the seed's "admin mutes the chat" beat.
- **Two-part mechanics:** end Part 1 on a sound or notification cue with the question unanswered. Title Part 2 so it can be found without Part 1, since payoff parts can outperform openers (topscaryclips 2/3 got 121x).
- **Kit needs:** #1 needs a lobby front-desk background with a desk phone. #10 needs a child puppet. #4 needs an emergency-alert banner, and #6 a delivery-app UI. Everything else uses the B4 backgrounds (door and peephole, hallway, elevator panel, bedroom, living room, stairwell) and the phone component.
- **Originality:** borrow structure only. Run `scripts/originality_check.py` against `research/corpus` and `research/library/transcripts` before any script is accepted.
