# Our Building Has 40 Units. The Group Chat Has 41 Members. (Part 1/2)

**Premise.** Larkspur Court has 40 flats and one residents' chat, one account per flat. The chat has 41
members. The extra one is called Night Security. Whenever someone asks "who's in the hallway?", it answers
first, within a second, and it is always right. The building has no night security and never has. At 1:12 a.m.
footsteps stop outside Nora's door (5B). Night Security says it's her neighbour Mr. Okafor (5A) with his
recycling, and through the peephole that's who she sees, except he doesn't blink and walks to the stairs instead
of home. Night Security messages her privately: it knows she looked. The neighbours like it; the admin can't get
rid of it. Then, for the first time, it answers a question nobody asked and names itself: Floor 8 stairwell.
Floor 7. Floor 6. Floor 5. The peephole goes black. Nora texts Mr. Okafor, who says he has been in bed since
nine and asks who she saw. The chat lights up: "@5B I'm at your door now, open up for your safety."

**Ownable rule / signature.** Two things the viewer can track: the count (40 units, 41 members) and the
ritual question "who's in the hallway?", which the extra member always answers first and correctly. Its own
pinned rules give the viewer a scorecard: (1) ask here, (2) never use your peephole, (3) if I come to your
door, open it. Rule 2 breaks twice, and rule 3 is the last line of Part 1.

**Plants.**
1. Rule 3 "If I come to your door, open it" (0:13) -> the final message (1:26). Paid.
2. Rule 2 "never use your peephole" (0:12) -> broken at 0:24, and "You looked" (0:34). Paid, then broken again at 1:03.
3. "Mr. Okafor... not blinking... walked to the stairs, not to his own door" (0:25-0:32) -> Okafor in bed since nine (1:19). Paid as the twist.
4. The stairs (0:29) -> "Floor 8 stairwell" (0:55). Paid.
5. **Unpaid for Part 2:** "214 answers since March, and it has never said where it is" (0:48); "removed six times, back every morning" (0:44); the 20-second door check (1:15, the cut lands before it runs out); who the 41st account is; 9D's cat that "it found".

**Loops.** L1 who is the 41st member (0:00, open); L2 is Night Security real (0:07, closed at 0:08: no, then re-opened as "what is it"); L3 who's in the hall at 1:12 (0:16 -> 0:21 "Okafor" -> re-opened 1:19, open); L4 how did it know I looked (0:34, open); L5 can it be removed (0:44, open); L6 where is it (0:51 -> 0:55 -> 1:01, closed: at my door); L7 will I open (1:26, open). **Five open at the cut** (L1, L3, L4, L5, L7), early loops closed before 50%, the right count for a series Part 1.
