## Paste block

You're strapped to a sled above the deepest trench on Earth, and it's about to drop. Three rules, three cheats, and an ALIVE? meter keep score all the way to Challenger Deep, 10,935 metres down. How many times does the ocean kill you on the way, and what got to the bottom before you?

Where would you have quit? Tell us the depth in the comments.

The trip is a thought experiment: nobody can breathe or survive unprotected at these depths, and the "cheats" are a game device. The animals, depths, pressures and records are real; sources below.
