## Claims the caption must not overstate

- **236 dB is underwater** (dB re 1 µPa at 1 m), not comparable to air decibels. Do not compare it to a jet engine, rocket or gunshot.
- **Eardrums are "on paper", untested.** The video says "could burst your eardrums" and "nobody's volunteered to check". Never write "will kill you", "deadly" or "would burst your eardrums".
- **The twist is that it is mostly harmless.** Full power is for hunting in the deep; near the surface they use softer codas. A "never swim near one" or "it could kill you" caption would be clickbait the video does not pay off.
- **"Loudest animal" means the loudest recorded animal sound** (Møhl et al. 2003). Fine as written; do not add "loudest sound on Earth".
- **Dominica swimmers' "thump in the chest"** is paraphrased testimony, not a measurement.
