## YouTube (16:9)

An adult male orca is about 8 metres long, weighs as much as four cars and can hit 56 km/h. Pods hunt as a team with their own calls, menus and plans:
they wash seals off ice floes, took the livers out of great white sharks off South Africa, and in 2019 brought down an adult blue whale off Western Australia.
There is still no confirmed case of a wild orca killing a person. The best explanation is culture: orcas eat what their mothers taught them.
Except, since 2020, orcas off Spain and Portugal have been ramming sailboats and snapping their rudders.
