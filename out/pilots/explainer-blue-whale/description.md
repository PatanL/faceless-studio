The blue whale is the heaviest animal ever measured: up to 30 metres long and close to 200 tonnes. That's longer than two city buses and heavier than any dinosaur we've dug up.
You would fit inside its mouth with room to spare. Its throat is only about as wide as a beach ball, though, so it physically can't swallow you. The real danger is being in the way when it turns.
