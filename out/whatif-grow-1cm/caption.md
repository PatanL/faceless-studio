## Claims the caption must not overstate

- **It is a hypothetical that waives the physics on purpose.** The video says your knees "should fold" under the square-cube law and then "They don't." Never write "what would really happen", "scientifically accurate" or "real science".
- **It stops at the Moon (11 years).** The source went on to planets and a black hole; ours does not. Do not promise "bigger than a planet", "bigger than the Sun" or anything past the Moon.
- **"As tall as the Moon is wide"**, not "taller than the Moon" or "reach the Moon" (the Moon is ~384,000 km away; you are ~3,474 km tall).
- **No death beat.** The air is "too thin to breathe" at Everest and there is "no air at all" in space, but the video never says you die or suffocate. Do not caption "you'd die at...".
- **Milestones are approximate** ("about ten days", "after four months"); keep them rounded and do not add exact times the video does not show.
