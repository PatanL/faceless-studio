## Claims the caption must not overstate

- **Not unbeatable.** The video says it "could beat anyone" and frame 0 reads "UNBEATABLE ROBOT", but the real Turk lost some games. Never write "never lost a game" or "undefeated".
- **The Napoleon and Franklin games are traditional accounts.** The illegal moves and the swept board come from 19th-century retellings. Keep them as "played Napoleon" or "beat Napoleon"; do not add details (dates, a quote, "Napoleon was furious") or call them documented fact.
- **People suspected a hidden player long before 1854** (e.g. Willis in 1821, Poe in 1836). The video says the secret "finally comes out" after the fire, meaning the full method was published (1857). Do not write "nobody figured it out for 84 years" or "no one ever suspected".
- **"Eighty years" is a round figure** (1770-1854, with idle years). Prefer "decades".
- **"Robot" is our hook word.** The word dates from 1920; in context lines call it an automaton or "the Turk", and never present it as a real early robot or early AI.
