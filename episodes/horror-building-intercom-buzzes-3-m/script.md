# The Intercom (Part 1)

**Premise.** At 3:07 a.m. the building intercom rings Nora's phone. A tired voice says it's June from 3B,
the flat directly below, locked out. The residents' chat says June moved out last Tuesday; Mr. Okafor
carried her sofa to the van. Nora tests the voice with her cat's name, which only June would know, and it
answers instantly. Then Priya, watching the lobby camera, says nobody is at the front door. The elevator
climbs to three, footsteps cross the empty flat beneath Nora, and the intercom rings again, this time from
Unit 3B: "I found my keys. I'm home now." Then the real June messages from another city: Nora's spare key
is still hanging by the door downstairs.

**Originality.** No killer, no fake officer, no muting admin, no burner phone, no count-match. The threat is
an impersonation through the intercom, the contradiction is a move-out, and the twist is a key that never
left the building. Part 2 plants: the spare key on June's ring, how the voice knew "Pickles", who still has
access to 3B.
