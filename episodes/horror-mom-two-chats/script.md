# Mom Is Outside (Part 1)

**Premise.** Mom posts in the family group chat that she's at the door with full hands. Seconds later she
texts privately: still at the hospital, don't open. Both pass a test question. Dad, who never uses the group,
vouches for the one outside. I call Dad, and his phone rings inside our hall closet. Dad left it at home.
The last frame: "Dad is typing..."

## Narration

```narration
[music: dread] At eleven at night, my mom posted in our family group chat. I'm outside. Unlock the door, my hands are full. [sfx: notify]

Ten seconds later, she texted me privately. Not in the group. Still at the hospital with Grandpa. Don't open the door for anyone.

Same name. Same photo. Two chats. One of them was lying.

So I asked the group: Mom, what was our first dog called? She answered straight away. Biscuit. That was right.

I asked the private chat the same thing. It took a whole minute. Then: Biscuit. Lock the deadbolt. Please.

The doorbell rang. [sfx: ding] Through the peephole I could see a shape holding grocery bags, head down, hood up.

Then Dad joined the group chat. He never uses it. He wrote: It's really her, sweetheart. Let her in. It's freezing out there.

So I called Dad to check. [sfx: phone_buzz_long @0.3]

It rang. Not on the line. In the house. His ringtone was coming from the hall closet, right behind me.

Dad had forgotten his phone this morning. He's at the hospital too. [pause: 0.3]

Which meant whoever just typed as Dad was in that closet.

The ringing stopped. And in the group chat, under Dad's name, three little dots appeared. Dad is typing. [sfx: stinger @0.2]
```
