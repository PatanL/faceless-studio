# Member 41 (Part 1)

**Premise.** The residents' chat has one more member than the building has flats: "Night Desk", no photo,
always first to answer and always right. Tonight it says nobody is in the stairwell while the stairwell door
stands open, the admin can't remove it, and the member count keeps rising. It orders the fourth floor to stay
in, single knocks move down the hall unit by unit, and it arrives at 4D, my door. The new 42nd member is
named 4D. Mine.

Distinct from the pilot: the villain is an account nobody added (counting device, like the "one extra
student" in omuJBFs2L6c), not a rules post; the threat moves door to door instead of one knock.
