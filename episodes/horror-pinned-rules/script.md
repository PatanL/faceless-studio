# The Pinned Rules (Part 1, PILOT)

**Premise.** At 1 a.m. a post signed "Management" pins seven rules in the residents' chat. Rule four: if
someone knocks three times, you are not home. A neighbour finds the management company was dissolved two
years ago. The loudmouth from 5A opens his door and goes silent. Then rule five is quietly edited, three
knocks land on my door, and a message from the man who opened his door says he's "right behind them".
The peephole shows only one person.

**Why this is the horror pilot.** It stacks the two strongest devices in the exact format: the rules hook
(udhwkz trucker rules 2.2M at 63x and 695K at 20x; WrONG Rules "Mom's Note" 31x) and the group-chat fake authority
(both seed outliers, 66.7x and 10.8x). It needs only existing kit sets (HandPhone chat, FrontDoor, Peephole,
Protagonist, Neighbour), and the plot shares no beats with the seed story (no killer, no fake cop, no muting, no burner).
Series potential: Part 2 = what the other six rules mean.
