# Two Rooms (Part 1)

**Premise.** My roommate texts from her bedroom: don't come out, there's someone in the living room who
looks like me. The living-room voice calls me for dinner. Both pass the knock test and the secret test. The
landlord says Jess moved out yesterday. Two doors open at once; the last text asks which one I'll let in.

Evidence: "my roommate isn't human" is a top outlier on two format accounts (mraevq 27.6x, melovebz 23.8x; backlog #5).
Distinct from the mom episode: both versions are in the flat, the authority (landlord) erases both, and the test
fails by both passing.

[music: tension_pulse] My roommate texted me from her bedroom. Don't come out. There's someone in the living room who looks like me. [sfx: notify]

But from the living room, I could hear her. Jess's voice, calling my name, saying dinner was ready.

I texted back. If you're really in your room, knock on the wall. [sfx: knock3 wait] Three knocks. From her side.

Then, from the living room: Who are you texting? Come and eat, it's getting cold.

So I asked the Jess on my phone something only she would know. What did I break last week? She wrote back. Your blue mug. That was right.

I asked the Jess in the living room the same thing, through my door. She laughed. Your blue mug, silly. Come out.

So I texted our landlord. Does anyone else have a key to our flat?

He replied straight away. Jess moved out yesterday. She handed in her keys this morning. Why, is someone there? [sfx: stinger @0.1 vol=-6]

Both of them went quiet. [pause: 0.5]

Then I heard two doors open at the same time. Her bedroom door. And the living room door, right outside mine. [sfx: door_creak]

My phone buzzed one last time. A text from Jess. Which one of us are you going to let in? [sfx: stinger @0.15]
