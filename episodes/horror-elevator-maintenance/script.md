# Don't Look At The Mirror (Part 1)

**Premise.** Stuck between floors at 1 a.m., I text the number on the emergency sticker. "Maintenance" gives a
ritual: press four, then two, hold close, never look at the mirror. The lift goes up instead of down. Then the
real maintenance company texts from another number: they're closed and my lift is out of service. Both
numbers tell me not to trust the other. The counter passes ten in a ten-floor building and the doors open on
a floor with no number. The last beat: my reflection isn't reading the phone.

Distinct engine: a one-to-one help line with a ritual (no group chat), and an elevator counter instead of a door.
Evidence: "The Elevator Game" 7.4M, Unfiled "Floor 13" 4.6x, udhwkz elevator story 17.6x (backlog #9).

## Narration

```narration
[music: dread] The elevator stopped between floors at one in the morning. Then the lights went out. [sfx: impact vol=-6]

There's a sticker by the buttons. In an emergency, text Maintenance. So I did.

They answered right away. Stay calm. Press four, then two, then hold the close button. Whatever you do, don't look at the mirror.

I'd never noticed the mirror before. In the dark, I could feel it behind me.

I pressed four. Then two. The elevator jolted, and started going up instead of down. [sfx: riser]

Maintenance texted again. Good. Keep holding close. You'll hear someone ask to get in. Say no.

Then a different number messaged me. It said it was building maintenance. We're closed until eight. Your elevator shows out of service. Who are you talking to? [sfx: notify]

Before I could answer, the first number wrote: Don't listen to them. They're the ones who want in.

The floor counter kept climbing. Eight. Nine. Ten. Eleven. Our building only has ten floors.

The elevator stopped. [sfx: ding] And the doors began to open, onto a floor with no number.

Both numbers texted me at the same time. One said: Run. The other said: Stay.

And out of the corner of my eye, in the mirror, my reflection wasn't reading the messages. It was looking straight at me. [sfx: stinger @0.15]
```
