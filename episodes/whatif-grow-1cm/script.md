# What Happens If You Never Stop Growing? (Short, lane C)

**Premise.** You grow one centimetre every second and never stop. Each rung of the ladder is a new kind of
problem, not just a bigger number: the room (a ceiling), the street (a car beside your shoe), physics (the
square-cube law), a city landmark, weather (clouds), the body (thin air), traffic (airliners), space (no air at
all), and astronomy (the Moon). The late reframe turns the clock on the viewer: if it started when they pressed
play, they'd be at the ceiling right now, which is frame 0.

**Spine loop:** "how big does this get?" Opens at 0:00 (the clock), each rung pays a little, closes on the Moon.
**Planted noun paid late:** "ceiling" (0:03, hook) comes back in the last line (~95%), and "one minute" becomes
the length of the video. The last frame is the frame-0 composition (the bedroom, head at the ceiling).
**Progress device (FEEDBACK 8):** a clock and height plate on every place shot; the height is always
1.75 m + 1 cm x the clock, so the picture is honest scale throughout.

## Facts to verify

- 1 cm/s = 0.6 m a minute, 36 m an hour, 864 m a day, ~315 km a year (arithmetic).
- Standard ceiling ~2.4 m; a 1.75 m person reaches it after ~65 s.
- After one hour: 1.75 + 36 = ~38 m, ~21.7x taller. A ten-storey building is ~30-35 m. A foot is ~15 % of height
  (5.7 m at 38 m) and a family car ~4.5 m long, so the car is smaller than your shoe.
- Square-cube law: scale length by 20 and weight (volume) scales by 20^3 = 8,000 while bone strength
  (cross-section) scales by 20^2 = 400 (Galileo, Two New Sciences, 1638; any intro biomechanics text).
- Nine hours: 1.75 + 324 = ~326 m. Eiffel Tower 330 m to the antenna tip (300 m as built). (toureiffel.paris)
- Day two: 0.86-1.73 km; cumulus cloud bases ~0.6-2 km (Met Office cloud guide).
- Everest 8,849 m (2020 China-Nepal survey) is reached after ~10.2 days. Air at that height has about a third of
  sea-level oxygen pressure: climbers call it the death zone (above ~8,000 m).
- Two weeks: ~12.1 km tall, chin ~87 % of height = ~10.5 km; airliners cruise at ~10-12 km (FAA / ICAO).
- Edge of space: the Karman line, 100 km (FAI), reached after ~116 days (just under four months).
- Moon diameter 3,474 km; 11 years = 3,469 km (11.02 years for the full width). Earth diameter 12,742 km, 3.7x.
- Video length ~63 s, so at the last line (~58-60 s in) the viewer would be ~60 cm taller.
