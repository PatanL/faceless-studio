# Beat map: "Would A Great White Survive Every Prehistoric Ocean?" (Depth Theory)

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mg2YJ04Hl9k · 19:41 · 157,498 views in 3.3 days · 1.12x channel median

Per-5s shot list with the narration for each window: `mg2YJ04Hl9k.shots.csv`. Frames: `../sheets/frames/mg2YJ04Hl9k_sheet01-08.jpg`.

This is a different format and art style from the "never want to meet" videos. It is a **tournament**: one familiar animal is dropped into each era and scored. Title, thumbnail ("Still King?" with a crown) and format closely follow Fossil's "Would Orcas Dominate Every Prehistoric Ocean?" (3.8M, 35x that channel's median).

## Per-era template (repeats 10 times)

1. Time-dial spin, then an era title card ("370 MILLION YEARS AGO / DEVONIAN PERIOD") with a creature swimming past (~5-10 s)
2. **World-building** (~20-30 s): flat landscape (land plants, oxygen, day length), map card of the continents, oxygen bar
3. **Meet the locals** (~20-40 s): each rival gets a name tag, then a **scale bar vs "our great white 5 m"**, often with a myth-bust ("old reconstructions 10 m?" vs "2023 study: 4.1 m")
4. **Fight logic** (~15-25 s): kinetic caption with one emphasised word ("a SNACK.", "in HALF.")
5. **Escape test**: the shark dives down a vertical depth gauge; does the rival follow? (777 m, 947 m, 1,200 m)
6. **Verdict** icon (crown/check/skull), plus a mini scoreboard in the corner
