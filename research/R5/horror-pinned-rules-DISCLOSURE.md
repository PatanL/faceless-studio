## AI-voice / altered-content disclosure (set on upload)

Checked against official platform pages on 2026-11-01 (lane R5); re-check the links on publish day.

- **What is synthetic:** the narration is a local text-to-speech voice (Kokoro-82M af_heart, Apache-2.0, not a clone of any real person).
  The story is original fiction; visuals are hand-coded 2D animation (no AI image/video models); music bed and SFX are procedurally
  synthesized by the channel (no samples, no claimed music).
- **YouTube / Shorts:** upload flow > Attributes > **"AI use" = Yes** (older Studio: "Altered content").
  YouTube requires disclosure only for content "that seems realistic", and fully animated video is exempt, but its rules name a synthetic
  voice narrating a video, and "Disclosing AI content alone won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn money."
  Expect a label in the expanded description, not on the player.
  Source: [Disclosing use of GenAI content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491),
  [YouTube Blog, 18 Mar 2024](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content/).
- **TikTok:** More options > **"AI-generated content" on** before posting. TikTok "requires people to label AI-generated content that
  contains realistic images, audio or video". Never remove or cover the label later.
  Source: [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/new-labels-for-disclosing-ai-generated-content),
  [Community Guidelines: Integrity and Authenticity](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity).
- **Instagram / Facebook Reels:** add the **AI label** ("AI info") when sharing. Meta requires its AI-disclosure tool for posts with
  "realistic-sounding audio" that was digitally created.
  Source: [Meta Community Standards: Misinformation](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/misinformation/).
- **Caption line (keep it on every platform):** "Original fiction. Narration uses a synthetic (AI) voice; all visuals are hand-coded 2D animation."
  It keeps the first-person "our building chat" story from being read as a real event or a real management company.
- **Monetisation:**
  - YouTube "inauthentic content" (renamed 15 Jul 2025) rejects "scrolling text with minimal or no narrative" and "readings of other
    materials you did not originally create": keep it original fiction with full narration and animated scenes; every Part needs new
    story beats; never re-upload the same cut with a new title.
    Source: [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392).
  - YouTube Shorts: "Shorts over one minute in length that contain claimed content are blocked." This cut is 65.2 s, so never add library
    or trending music. Source: [Shorts monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220).
  - TikTok Creator Rewards: videos must be "at least 1 minute" (65.2 s clears it; keep any re-cut above ~62 s) and original.
    Source: [Creator Rewards Program Terms (US)](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en).
  - Ad suitability: dramatized, non-graphic dread earns full ads; avoid "severe and shocking injuries" and gory thumbnails.
    Source: [Advertiser-friendly content guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278).
