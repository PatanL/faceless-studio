## Visual grammar notes

- **Character design:** stock-style realistic 3D humans (mid-poly, slightly uncanny, readable expressions). The everyman protagonist wears **one bright flat colour** (salmon-pink tee, dark shorts) so the eye finds him even as a dot in top-down shots. The antagonist gets the complementary colour (green tee) plus one prop (a hat) to read as a "type". Crowds are instanced extras in muted clothes. Faces carry the emotion: grimace, relief, deadpan, bewilderment.
- **Palette:** saturated cyan-blue sky gradient, green-brown grass, glossy white and grey hardware. Accents carry meaning: pink = the hero, green = positive UI, red = alarm or cost, cyan = machine LEDs, **purple tint = machine POV**. The phone UI is dark navy and black with the brand's blue.
- **Lighting:** flat, bright midday HDRI light with soft shadows and ambient occlusion. There is no mood lighting; everything is clear at a glance. Mood changes come from colour grades (the purple HUD), not from the lights.
- **Materials:** product hardware looks like consumer tech (glossy white plastic or ceramic, chrome pole, emissive cyan rings). The gross fluid is rendered **amber and orange with particle splashes**, cartoony rather than realistic, so the gag reads as funny rather than disgusting. The X-ray cutaway uses a translucent shell.
- **Camera language:** it switches between extreme angles. God's-eye top-down shots show scale and space; low heroic angles show the product; macro inserts show interfaces (phone, QR code, lens, nozzle); purple HUD POV shots show what the machine "thinks". Every shot moves (push, pull, drift, orbit). Transitions use whip pans and motion blur. Wide lenses, with near-fisheye skies in the flight shots.
- **Cut rhythm:** about 37 shots in 45 s, a new image roughly every 1.2 s. Cuts land on clause boundaries, usually one shot per 2-3 caption chunks. Holds stretch to 2-3 s only for the HUD lock-on, the slow-motion spray, the look-away tableau and the rating UI.
- **Caption style:** burned-in, **2-3 words per chunk** (each on screen 0.4-1.0 s), white bold sans-serif with a thin dark outline and soft shadow, no box, no keyword colours, no emoji, sentence case. Small (about 4% of frame height), centred low at about 78% of frame height, and in the same place in every shot, including the full-screen UI shots.
- **Branding:** a fake in-world company logo stays consistent across every phone screen, and the channel name appears on a set piece as an easter egg. Use our own equivalents and never copy theirs.
