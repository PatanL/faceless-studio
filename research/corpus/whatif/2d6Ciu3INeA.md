## Transferable skeleton (no original wording)

For a "What if you [changed by a tiny constant rate] every second?" Short, ~2:45–2:55, ~150 wpm, ~430 words, single narrator.

1. **Hook, 0–5%** (~25–30 words, ~9 s, fastest pace). One sentence states the premise with a tiny unit and "you". Then two time-stamp rungs, each ×60 (a minute, an hour), with maths the viewer can check. End on a familiar everyday comparison object.
2. **First consequence, ~6%**. The comparison object plus "but" and one physical side effect people can see or hear.
3. **Real-science off-ramp, ~8%**. Name one real principle ("something called…"), then a rule-of-three list of how it would really go wrong. Make it sound like the video could end here (honest fake ending).
4. **Pivot, ~3%** (≤16 words). Dismiss reality in a few words, add a brand or format line, and promise to push the premise to its maximum (this opens the master loop).
5. **Spectacle act, ~11%**. A genre label for the new "you", one landmark, one "X are like Y to you" relative-size flip, then crowd and authority reactions, in short lines.
6. **Landmark ladder, ~12%**. Three rungs, each "time stamp + famous tall thing", going from built world to nature. Add one line about what the new size lets you do (power fantasy) between rungs.
7. **Threshold act, ~10%**. A sensory body cost (breathing, cold), then a 3–4-word "still going" re-hook, then crossing a major boundary at a precise odd time stamp (~50% mark) shown as a split image.
8. **Planetary act, ~14%**. "Fast forward" plus a big time skip; comparisons switch to celestial bodies. Add an identity line ("no longer…"), a second real-physics mechanism, and a short role-reversal punch.
9. **Stellar act, ~13%**. A second "doesn't stop" re-hook (~70%). Put two rungs in one line to speed up, show harm that grows with scale, and repeat the relative-size flip from step 5 one level up.
10. **Finale, ~17%**. Absurd time numbers. Identity reframe "not just X, you are Y". The largest real measurement with an "if somehow" hedge. A philosophical sting that breaks off on a joining word, so the replay's first line finishes it.

Pacing rules: a new time stamp or comparison object every ~10 s (never more than ~30 s without one, and only when a science or pivot beat fills the gap). A turn-phrase re-hook every 30–40 s, each starting a new scale domain. Milestones spaced on a log scale of size so the clock speeds up by itself. Get every number right (viewers will check). One honest real-science off-ramp early (by ~20%) and one later physics nod. No CTA; the loop is the CTA.
