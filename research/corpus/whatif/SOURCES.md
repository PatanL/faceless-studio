# What-if corpus: sources (12 viral outliers)

Built 2026-11-01 ~09:05 UTC by the whatif corpus gatherer. Pool: R3 (`research/R3/report.md`, `raw/r3_videos.json`) and `research/library/candidates-whatif.json` (65 videos across Awaken, IFOLOGY, @man_ia_c, plus the adjacent channels ConChaser, Oddly Elsewhere and OminiAM). YouTube views were re-pulled today. TikTok views come from the R3 pull (~08:45 UTC).

**Outlier score** = views ÷ the channel's median for the same format (long-form vs Shorts; TikTok uses all 22 posts). Every channel here is 1–4 weeks old, so medians are small and still moving. Read the ×100 scores (OminiAM) as "a breakout on a tiny channel", not as a size comparison with the others.

**Transcript method**
- YouTube: `~/.local/bin/yt-dlp --skip-download --write-auto-subs --sub-langs en-orig --sub-format vtt` on the Spark. Rolling captions were deduplicated with `dgx-spark:~/studio/refs/corpus/whatif/vtt.py`, which keeps the newly timed line of each cue, drops the 10 ms flash cues and merges fragments into sentences. Each line reads `[mm:ss] sentence`, and `—` marks a speaker change.
- TikTok (@man_ia_c): the videos have no speech. faster-whisper small (CPU, 4 threads, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=""`) returned no words, so they are **caption-only**. Frames were sampled at 1 fps on the Spark, and caption-band contact sheets (`dgx-spark:~/studio/refs/corpus/whatif/sheets/`) were read visually. Each on-screen element is transcribed with a timestamp: TITLE, CAPTION, HUD counter/subline, END card. The post caption (description) is included as a comment line.
- Every file starts with the line `# url | title | channel | views | outlier_score | duration`.

## Shorts template (9:16, ~80 s): "X disappeared for N seconds" (@man_ia_c)

The same skeleton repeats across all five, and every element of it can be built in code:
- A title card over the place (0–5 s), with a pre-event HUD countdown.
- "You're in [named place]." (5 s)
- At 8 s the HUD flips to "RETURNS IN 0:04.9 / TIME SLOWED DOWN 11×".
- A new one-line caption every ~4 s. Each caption is one consequence, and the scale escalates from body → city → planet → Moon/galaxy. HUD sublines carry a live number ("YOUR WEIGHT: 41 LB", "EARTH IS NOW 402,676 MILES WIDE").
- "BACK" beat at ~64 s with a twist: the return makes it worse.
- Black at ~72 s, then an end card with a real fact in small caps and a comment CTA.

## Expansion (12:08 UTC)

Added 2026-11-01 12:08–12:25 UTC by the whatif corpus expansion gatherer: **13 new transcripts** in `raw/` (no annotated `.md` and no INDEX.json rows yet). **How they were found:** `ytsearch40` on the Spark for "what if you", "what if earth", "what if shorts", "what if the sun disappeared" and "what if humans". After that, the recent uploads of each candidate channel were listed (`--flat-playlist --playlist-end 60`, videos and shorts tabs, pulled ~12:10 UTC) to get a same-format median. **Outlier score** = views ÷ that median. For channels already in the corpus, the medians from the 11:08 daily pull (`research/library.jsonl`) were used. The established channels (What If 8.99M subs, xkcd 1.38M, WatchData 1.69M, SolarBalls 2.28M) have large medians, so a 4–10× there means far more absolute reach than the same score on a 1-week-old channel. Three of the picks (AV1zVKuEtFc from 2022, eiBR6wlwPfA from 2023, 8fADp43wJwU from 2024) are older than their channel's 60-upload window, so they are scored against today's median. No ordinary controls were added this round; every pick is ≥4×.
