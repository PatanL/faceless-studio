## Transferable skeleton

A caption-only, POV "giant/scale" what-if in ~80 s. No wording from the source.

1. **0–7% Hook card.** On-screen question "What if [ordinary thing] was [N]x [bigger/faster/heavier]?" over a shot that already shows the answer. A HUD counter starts on frame 1: label = the threat, number = distance to the viewer, counting down.
2. **7–23% Setup.** Caption 1 restates the premise relative to "you". Captions 2–3 give a specific-sounding mechanism (a real scientific term + one number). Leave the term unexplained (open loop).
3. **23–37% Stakes as numbers.** Swap the HUD subline to a new stat between captions. Each caption converts one stat into a famous reference object or the setting itself (heavier than X, wider than Y).
4. **37–44% First damage, silent.** No caption; the subject touches the environment. Breaks the rhythm.
5. **~44% Relocation re-hook.** Hard cut to a specific, mundane place where "you" are (a numbered floor, a room, a seat). Reset the HUD: new label = one part of the subject, number = distance to *that place*, plus a location tag the viewer doesn't understand yet.
6. **44–74% Escalation ladder, 4–6 captions.** Alternate: a speed/force number, a sensory detail (sound, vibration at your window), a dark-humor mundane line that plants a bystander, then the biggest force comparison. Each line 4–9 words, one every ~4–5 s. The counter keeps dropping.
7. **74–78% Reveal.** One caption explains the location tag: you are exactly where the impact lands. Counter hits ~1.
8. **78–92% Payoff, silent.** Counter label becomes "HERE". 8–12 s of destruction from inside the room, no text.
9. **~92% Black frame**, 1 s.
10. **92–100% End card.** Repeat the title question; one small line confirming the real science from step 2 (closes the loop, gives "wait, that's real?" comments); CTA asking viewers to pick the next what-if.

Pacing targets from this reference: hook card ≤ 8 words for ~6 s; ~11 captions of 4–9 words; mean caption gap ~4.5–5.5 s; one silent action beat before the relocation and one long silent payoff at the end; two countdowns (approach, then precise target).
