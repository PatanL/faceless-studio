## Transferable skeleton

No original wording. Times assume a 60-90 s short; scale proportionally.

1. **0-10% Premise + pre-event clock.** Open with the "What if [one fundamental thing] [changed] for [short fixed duration]?" question and one highlighted keyword. A HUD countdown to the event starts at frame 1. Plant one quiet fact line that the ending will pay off. Show a calm, recognizable real place, and address the viewer as "you" in that place.
2. **10-25% Event + harmless first effect.** The clock hits zero and resets as a "returns in" countdown with a slow-motion multiplier (makes a few seconds of story fill most of the runtime). First consequence is odd and personal, converted into a stat about "you" with a relatable comparison (weight, size, age). Add a POV shot of the viewer's own body.
3. **25-35% The "But" turn.** One line explains the real mechanism the thing was holding up, and the scope jumps from you to everything. The HUD subline swaps to a frightening per-unit number (energy per gram, etc.) before the visuals show it.
4. **35-55% Escalation, human scale.** Hard cut or colour shift as a pattern interrupt. Two or three rising numeric beats (speed, energy per person), each line 6-8 words, 3 s on screen, with text-free gaps between. Peak on a shareable "each person becomes X" line, then a white-out that wipes to the next scale.
5. **55-65% Escalation, planetary.** Cut to a wide/space view. One headline number and a live counter that climbs every second.
6. **65-80% Cliffhanger race.** Introduce a second threat with its own countdown (next body or target to be hit). Let the two clocks race with almost no captions for 10-15 s. The main clock wins by a hair.
7. **80-90% Return + ironic twist.** The thing comes back. Callback to the opening place with a "[place], [duration] later" stamp. Show the ghost/aftermath version of the opening shot. The twist: undoing the change doesn't undo the damage. Pay off the fact planted in step 1, then give one longer-timescale consequence number.
8. **90-100% Black beat + bookend card.** About 1-2 s of black, then repeat the title question with the same highlighted keyword, plus a two-line takeaway fact ("X is N% of you / without it, Y"). Hold ~6 s for the loop. Put the "which one next?" ask in the post caption, not the video.
