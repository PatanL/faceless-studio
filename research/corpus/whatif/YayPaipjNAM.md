- Cold open 0:00–0:03 (3 s): a flash-forward frame from 1:27 + a 4-word premise caption + HUD. Title card at 0:03 (8 words). First story caption at 0:10.

## Transferable skeleton

A caption-only, POV, continuous-scale what-if at 16:9, ~6.5–7 min. No wording from the source.

1. **0–1% Cold open.** Flash-forward to the strangest image from ~20% in (a creature or object towering over "you"), HUD already showing a tiny number + "smaller/bigger than ___". One 3–5-word caption that states the premise as a fact about "you" with no off switch.
2. **1–7% Title + fast run.** Title card in an ordinary home room at normal size. The counter starts moving on frame 1. One short line gives a vague cause and never explains it. 3–4 HUD comparison rungs (pet, coin, insect) in ~20 s; one "it's still going" caption. Then slow the counter down.
3. **7–26% World 1 + creature threat 1.** Name the first micro-world in one line. One metaphor caption ("each X is a Y now"). One true, slightly gross fact. "Something" line → silent reveal of a small creature. A pair of parallel short lines: you match it, then you don't. A near-miss line. Close the cold-open loop by showing that shot. A "something above" sensory cue → silent hit → **cut to black** (fake death).
4. **26–44% World 2 + threat 2.** Cut in on the new world after the black; one line says how the last threat pushed you here. Label the new objects in 2–3-word captions. Second "something is coming" threat that mistakes you for something else; plain stakes line; trapped line + engulf visual (fake death 2). **Twist: the premise saves you** ("but" line: you are now too small/big for it to hurt you). Escape line.
5. **44–58% World 3, wonder.** Black cut + new world. Location line, a sensory line, a label line, an unexplained sensory mystery, then its real explanation plus a famous name and year. One "now you're smaller than it" reversal.
6. **58–72% World 4, counter-intuition.** Use a different cut colour (white-out) to mark leaving the familiar domain. Two myth-busting lines in a row (what it isn't, what it is). A two-word "still going" re-hook (~67%). One famous counter-intuitive fact that explains why the screen is nearly empty, then ~9 s of silent near-black with one point of light.
7. **72–85% World 5, speed-up.** One-word reveal caption. Exact real numbers in fragments. Two HUD rungs in ~10 s. Let the HUD switch to a new unit system (powers of ten) as a silent re-hook. One line about the limit of measurement.
8. **85–98% The unknown.** The HUD comparison becomes a limit ("smaller than anything ever ___"). Admit nobody knows; echo the first "something lives here" line with no answer this time. One hedged real hypothesis told as an image; then "the rules stop here". Counter lands on the real limit value; label becomes "here" over black.
9. **98–100% End card.** Repeat the title. One two-number comparison that puts "you" between the smallest and largest scales and turns the trip back on the viewer. No CTA.

Pacing targets from this reference: hook ≤ 12 words of text in ~10 s; ~50 captions of 1–8 words (mean ~4.6), one every ~7–8 s; a HUD comparison rung every ~20–25 s (denser in the first 30 s and last 90 s); a macro re-hook (new world, cut or threat) every ~30 s, never more than ~60 s apart; two fake deaths in the first 44%; black cuts between worlds, one white cut at the biggest domain jump; every number real and checkable.
