**Key mechanic:** the premise is a single refusal ("you never come out") run forward on a **clock that keeps accelerating** (minutes → days → weeks → months → years → a century) while the cards arrive at a **fixed 3.5-s beat**. Because the card rate is constant and the time steps keep growing, each card covers more story time than the last, so the stakes climb by themselves. The story is told in second person to the baby ("you"), and the baby turns from comic nuisance (0:06 "evil baby" plant) into a monster: comedy for the first half, a body-horror and possession turn at 1:34 (52 %), society-level panic from 2:12, a mob kills "you" at 2:48, and a 100-year epilogue erases you. Real-life anchors (fetal milestones, pregnancy bladder issues, "terrible twos", pre-school age) sit on the early cards so the absurd later cards feel earned.

**Plausibility note:** fiction by design (the disclaimer says so). The few real anchors are loose: babies lift their heads at ~2 months after birth, the "terrible twos" are a toddler phase, pre-school starts at ~3-4 years (the card puts it at 8). The video never pretends to be science; it is a dark comedy that borrows the look of an infographic.

## Transferable skeleton (no original wording)

For a "Timeline: What if [you] [never stopped doing X / refused a normal life step]?" video as **scrolling text cards with no narration**, ~3:00, ~50 cards at a fixed ~3.5 s each, ~5-word bold titles + ~14-word bodies + one flat literal icon per card, music bed only.

1. **Countdown hook, 0-6 %** (4 cards, BEFORE badges). A cheeky first card, a card that casts the viewer as "you" with the reveal on the last word, a calm "normal" baseline card, then one rude or impossible image that labels "you" as trouble (the plant that pays off later).
2. **Clock zero + normal bait, 6-12 %** (3 cards: START, minutes, hours). The expected event is due; one "any moment now" card; the first sign it is late, as a question title.
3. **Refusal + baffled experts, 12-18 %** (3 cards, days → 1 week). Name the mystery ("nobody knows why"), make it a choice ("you refuse"), quote an unnamed expert saying it has never happened.
4. **Fix fails absurdly, 18-26 %** (4 cards, weeks → months). One serious fix ("this should work"), then a cartoon-logic reason it fails (caps "Wait,"), then a visible size or body change and a real-world milestone it parallels.
5. **Life around the problem, 26-37 %** (6 cards, months). A gross-but-relatable real fact pushed to extreme, a family member leaves, the problem goes public (livestream/news), world fame, a pun card, a "they suffer / you're relaxed" contrast card.
6. **Act break, ~37 %** (unit jump to years). A card that ends on "you're going to want more..." to open act 2.
7. **You grow and act, 37-52 %** (7 cards). Self-awareness, a real-phrase twist (age stage name), the first violent break-out image, then an immediate comic gross-out card, a missed normal-life milestone, "you love the attention / they don't".
8. **Genre turn, ~52 %.** One card where a witness reacts to something demonic; the tone switches to horror from here.
9. **Power flip + takeover, 52-73 %** (10-11 cards). Concrete harm with named body parts, a chirpy "No X? No problem" card on a horrible image, the other side tries to remove you, an absurd legal/consumer-product card (lawyer, games console) as relief, then "you take full control" with the card-1 icon character returning shocked. Mid-roll subscribe pop-up right after this peak (~66 %). The victim fades over 2-3 short cards.
10. **World reacts, 73-85 %** (6 cards). A funeral/disowning, fear spreading to everyone like them, a society-level behaviour change (a nested "what if"), you loose in public on the news, one vice joke, then the plainest, most grotesque one-line image.
11. **Hunt and end, 85-95 %** (5 cards). A card that names the genre shift out loud, a monster label, every institution unites, the crowd arrives (hear, then see), destruction "once and for all".
12. **Century skip, ~95 %** (1 card). A huge time jump to an erasure epilogue with an ironically calm icon.
13. **End card, ~4 %.** "Could you imagine?" question + comment ask + animated subscribe; optional short spoken outro.

Pacing rules: one card every 3-4 s with no gaps; a time-unit jump every ~8-30 s early and a final century jump at ~95 %; half-step badges (x.5) mixed in so the scale never feels mechanical; a major re-hook (unit jump, new character or institution, shock image, tone shift) every ≤10 s after the first 20 %; a comic-relief card within 1-2 cards after each shock; ≤1 question and ≤1 exclamation per card; titles ≤8 words and readable alone, bodies ≤19 words and optional; the 3 %-in plant must pay off in ≥4 stages; circle of impact widens from one person to all of society by ~80 %.
