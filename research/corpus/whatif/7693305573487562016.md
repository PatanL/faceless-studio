## 3. Transferable skeleton

Template for a caption-only (or VO) "what if X vanished/changed for N seconds" sim, about 75–85 s. Structure only, no wording.

1. **Premise card + pre-event clock (0–10%).** Title poses a what-if with a tiny duration. A HUD clock counts down to the event over an ordinary baseline scene. A baseline readout (a normal measurement) is visible so the change can be shown as a delta.
2. **Place the viewer (5–8%).** One second-person line puts "you" in a familiar, specific location with other people and one visual "tell" object that will react first.
3. **Event, no words (~10%).** The clock hits zero, the tell object changes silently, the HUD flips to a "returns in" counter running in heavy slow motion (about 9x), and the readout shows a percentage drop.
4. **First consequence = body sensation (~12%).** The smallest, most universal physical effect, then one rule line with the governing statistic that explains it.
5. **Escalation ladder, 4–5 rungs, about 5 s each (~35%).** Each rung is a stat line ("this everyday thing is N% of the missing element") followed by a consequence line. Go outward in scale (machines → nature → buildings), and make the last rung the ground or body under "you". On the second rung, plant a by-product that stays around (Chekhov's gun) and give it its own rising HUD meter. Swap the meter whenever a caption gap runs past 6 s.
6. **False calm (~10%).** A reassuring line with a time hedge ("for now…") that quietly promises a reversal.
7. **Turn (~5%).** A "then" line as the master clock reaches zero: the element returns.
8. **Twist + approach (~6%).** The planted by-product reacts with the returning element. A new distance countdown toward "you" and an equation label with the result left blank.
9. **Payoff (~10%).** The countdown reaches "here", the equation completes with a surprising-but-true result, and the visuals swing to the result.
10. **Black beat, then end card (~13%).** Repeat the title so the replay loops cleanly. Add one quotable fact line with a second statistic tying the element to "your" body or ground. Put the engagement question ("would you have…?") in the post caption, not on screen.

Pacing targets: ≤7 words per caption, ~5 s hold, ~60 wpm reading pace inside the story window, at least one moving number on screen at all times, at least 3 overlapping open loops, and the master loop spanning more than half the runtime.
