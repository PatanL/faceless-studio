- **Hard hook:** 1 sentence, 9 words, 2.9 s. It combines a superlative ("most dangerous") with an unexpected kind of consequence (a mental effect instead of death).
- **Extended hook** (through the pivot): 40 words, 0:00-0:10.3. The subject is not named until 0:10 (18% in). The misdirect list buys those seconds with jokes instead of filler.

### Re-hook interval
- **Big re-hooks:** 0:08 (pivot) and 0:31 ("not the worst part"), 23 s apart. The second one sits at 53%, right after the most graphic image.

### Escalation ladder
Each rung goes either more personal or more final:
1. Abstract potency (x100 cobra, x1000 tarantula)
2. Body symptoms (cramps, burning, vomiting)
3. Visible marks (branded-skin simile, then the scar photo)
4. **Mind** (feeling of doom, patients begging to die): the peak, closes the hook
5. Speed (all of it within 5 minutes)
6. Death mechanism (lose control while swimming, sink)
7. Worst physical damage (brain bleed, glossed plainly)
8. Off-ramp (survivable if treated, but trauma for life)

Note that the emotional peak (4) comes before the literal death (6). The writer pays off the hook's promise first, then keeps raising physical stakes so the back third doesn't sag after the payoff.

### How it ends
A relief line with a "but" (you survive, you're scarred for life), then a callback joke to the Australia framing and an insult-metaphor for the animal ("biggest op of all"). There is no CTA and no "follow for more". The last word cuts hard into the loop, so a replay lands on the hook claim again.

### Pacing takeaways for our explainer Shorts
- About 275 wpm is the ceiling for a TikTok-native fact Short. For TTS, aim lower (~210-240 wpm) so it stays clear, but keep pauses under 0.7 s.
- 1 sentence = 1 caption card, about 14 words. Change something on screen every 3-4 s even when the background holds.
- The subject's name can wait about 10 s if the wait is filled with jokes or a misdirect list.

## 3. Transferable skeleton (no original wording)

Target: 55-60 s, 9:16, ~220-260 words, 18-20 sentences, 1 caption card per sentence.

1. **Hook claim, 0-5% (~8-10 words).** "[Superlative] [creature/thing] in [place] can [unexpected kind of consequence]." Pick a consequence from a category nobody links to the subject (mind, memory, time, identity, not just "kills you"). This opens the main loop. *Visual:* creator/mascot close-up, with the claim as a caption card.
2. **Misdirect list, 5-14%.** Three or four famous threats of the same place or category, one of them absurd or funny. It sets up "everyone knows these" and earns a laugh. *Visual:* a collage of 3-4 icons.
3. **Pivot, 14-17% (one short line).** "But the worst one isn't [where you'd look]." This is the first re-hook. *Visual:* same collage, new caption (only the text changes).
4. **Reveal + paradox, 17-26%.** Name the subject and state the size/appearance-vs-danger contrast, then put "you" in its path ("you'd never notice it"). *Visual:* the subject next to an everyday object for scale (coin, fingertip).
5. **Ladder rung 1: numbers, 26-38%.** "When it gets you, ..." then a two-step multiplier against animals people already fear (x[N] worse than A, x[10N] than B). *Visual:* same scale shot, mascot jumps sides.
6. **Ladder rung 2: body list, 38-47%.** Signpost ("Next/Then"), then 4-5 quick physical effects in one breath. *Visual:* a big hero close-up of the subject.
7. **Ladder rung 3: proof you can see, 47-53%.** A testimony ("people describe it as ...") with a sensory simile, then "and that's what it actually looks like" as you show the evidence. *Visual:* an evidence card (illustrated marks/diagram; no real injury footage).
8. **Main re-hook + hook payoff, 53-70%.** "That's still not the worst part." Then explain the strange consequence from the hook, ending on an extreme, human reaction attributed to cases or reports. This is the emotional peak. *Visual:* the most striking hero image, held the longest (~10 s).
9. **Ladder rung 4-5: speed + mechanism, 70-81%.** "All of that in as little as [short time]", then a specific "if you're [doing X] when it hits..." scenario that ends in the worst plain outcome. *Visual:* timer/countdown plus a scenario graphic.
10. **Ladder rung 6: worst damage + plain gloss, 81-88%.** "It can even cause [technical term], meaning [blunt plain restatement]." *Visual:* a new close-up of the subject or an anatomy icon.
11. **Relief with a sting, 88-93%.** "[Fix] in time and you live, but [lasting cost]." *Visual:* the subject shown tiny again (in a jar or on a fingertip), bringing back the "tiny" promise.
12. **Button / callback, 93-100%.** Call back to the place/category frame from step 2, then a funny insult-nickname for the subject's shape or role. Hard cut with no CTA so the video loops into step 1.

- Use our own subject, numbers and jokes, and check every fact.
- Keep the "odd consequence" idea, but avoid self-harm or suicide wording in our hooks. It forces algospeak and risks limited ads (see R5). Use other mental or perceptual effects (time distortion, paralysis while awake, hallucination, memory loss) as the strange category.
- Code-only version: replace the green-screen presenter with the explainer kit's stick-figure "YOU" or a mascot that switches sides at each rung. Draw the stills as SVG cards (scale-vs-object, symptom icon list, multiplier bars, countdown).
