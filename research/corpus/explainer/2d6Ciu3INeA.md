- **Re-hook interval:** two layers. (a) Micro: a new time stamp or landmark about every 9 s (18 landmark comparisons and 13 time jumps in about 170 s). Every rung is a small reward. ## 3. Transferable skeleton (no original wording)

Template: **"Tiny constant rate, taken to the cosmic limit"** (second-person scale ladder). Length 60-170 s, about 150-430 words at about 150 wpm, one rung per 8-10 s.

1. **Premise line (0-2 s).** Repeat the title question in second person with a small, harmless-sounding rate or quantity ("what if you [did X] at [tiny unit] per [short time]"). Visual: you at normal scale plus a counter.
2. **Two fast proofs (2-8 s, about 5%).** Two time jumps with exact numbers, each about 10-60x the last. Use "just/already" to make small numbers feel fast. This teaches the format.
3. **First landmark + first cost (about 6%).** Compare with a familiar everyday structure, and show one small piece of damage to the world.
4. **Real-science check (about 8%).** Name one real law that makes the premise fatal or impossible, and list three body or system failures. It gives credibility and works as a fake ending.
5. **Waiver re-hook (about 3%, 3 short lines).** Drop the realistic outcome on purpose, name the format or channel as the deal, and restate the open question: "how far does this go?"
6. **Human-scale act (about 11%).** A pop-culture identity label, one landmark, one POV scale flip (small thing becomes tiny), then a damage ladder of property, people and institutions.
7. **Record ladder (about 15%).** One record holder per sentence (tallest structure, tallest building, tallest natural feature), ended by one bodily sensation line.
8. **Midpoint re-hook (about 50%).** A 3-5 word "it keeps going" line, then cross a boundary (atmosphere, ocean floor, orbit) with a strange split-body picture and a list of three hazards.
9. **Speed-up + identity shift (about 60-65%).** "Fast forward" to a bigger time unit. Match a moon, then your home planet (a full-circle moment), then "you are no longer [category]". Add a second real-physics beat that now comes from you (you have gravity, your own heat).
10. **Cosmic acceleration (about 70-90%).** A "doesn't stop" re-hook. Put two rungs in one sentence ("X, then Y"), raise the damage to the system level, and repeat the step-6 POV flip one level up (planets as small things). Repeat the identity shift one level up.
11. **Limit reveal (about 90%).** The largest real measure, stated exactly. Close the open question.
12. **Verdict + loop cut (last 5%).** "You wouldn't just [be in X], you'd [be X]", cut on a connective that runs back into the premise line. No CTA.

Rules to carry over:
- Every sentence moves at least one ladder: time, size comparison, damage, identity. Never two sentences in a row without a new number or landmark before the 90% reveal.
- Keep second person all the way through. Use body sensations early and identity lines late.
- Short "still going" re-hooks (3-8 words) about every 30 s, placed right after a damage beat.
- Repeat a device one level up (scale flip, identity shift, list of three) instead of repeating wording.
- Do the math for real at every rung (unlike the source's loose cosmic numbers). Exact numbers are the credibility engine. Put a running counter on screen.
- End on the biggest true measure, then a one-line identity verdict that loops into frame 0.
