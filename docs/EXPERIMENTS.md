# Experiment ledger

Every posted Short is a test. Before posting, write what it tests and what we expect; at 48 h and 7 d, fill in the
numbers and what we learned. Two kinds of evidence feed the loop:

- **References (prior):** proven viral videos. Learn first principles from them by comparing each channel's outliers
  with its own ordinary videos (same channel, same style, different result), so a principle isn't just "something
  winners happen to share" (research/library/INDEX.md does this for explainers).
- **Our posts (posterior):** viewed-vs-swiped-away rate, average % viewed, the retention curve's drop points, loops,
  shares. These confirm or overturn what the references suggested for *our* channel.

Lock rule (owner): keep testing lanes until one video reaches 10K views, then lock to that style.
